Privacy Policy & Data Compliance

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

At AdAudit.xyz, we take your advertising data security and privacy very seriously. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, and safeguard your information when you utilize our autonomous AI ad compliance and auditing platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Work email addresses and secure authentication identifiers handled securely via Supabase and Google OAuth protocols.

  • Google & Meta Ads Performance Data: When professional ad accounts are connected, we access campaign performance metrics, ad text copy, and diagnostic parameters strictly to execute AI compliance scans, pre-publish risk scoring, and budget leakage detection.

3. How We Use Collected Data

The information we collect is strictly utilized for authenticating users, maintaining secure session states, executing automated AI ad compliance scans, generating real-time risk diagnostic reports, and preventing daily advertising budget leakage.

4. Data Security & Encryption

We deploy robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including industry-standard 256-bit TLS encryption—to protect your personal information and campaign metrics against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell, trade, or rent your personally identifiable information or ad account metrics to external third parties. Data sharing is strictly restricted to essential certified infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud, Google APIs, and Supabase) under strict confidentiality agreements.
